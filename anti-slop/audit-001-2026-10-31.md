# Anti-slop audit 001 (31 Oct 2026)

Mode: AFTER. Scope: Expo app (`app/src`, `app/App.tsx`), website and API copy (`api/src/site.ts`, `api/src/server.ts`), demo seed data (`api/src/seed.ts`).
Method: code inspection plus the web build click-through done earlier today (login, Discover, vendor page, enquiry chat, Plan overview and checklist, calendar picker, guests, vendor workspace). Nothing below has been changed yet.

Design read: Indian wedding marketplace and planner for couples and their families, in a wedding-stationery style (ivory paper, burgundy, gold, Fraunces display serif), dials ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-02 em dashes on the website and in an API message.** `site.ts`: page titles "Privacy Policy — Shaadi OS", "Terms & Conditions — Shaadi OS", "Page not found — Shaadi OS"; "Placeholder draft — have a lawyer review…" (×2); "(placeholder — set your real officer…)"; the Terms sentence "Misuse — including … fraudulent listings — may result…". `server.ts:108` RSVP error "That response didn't look right — …". Two code comments also use them (`site.ts:4`, `server.ts:200`).
   Fix: replace with a colon, comma, period or parentheses; titles become "Privacy Policy | Shaadi OS" to match the home page title.
2. **R-38 / R-18 fictional vendors shown with a "Verified" badge.** All 12 seed vendors are invented businesses (`seed.ts` inserts `verified = 1` for every one). The app shows them in Discover and on vendor pages with a green Verified seal and no sign that they are samples. The website says "Listings may contain sample data", but the app does not.
   Fix: seed them with `verified = 0` and show a small "Sample listing" tag on seed vendors in Discover and on the vendor page until real vendors are onboarded.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

3. **R-05 / R-14 website home page is the hero + three identical cards template.** "Find your people / Make a plan / Celebrate together": same size, icon, padding and one-line copy, plus a rule-of-three heading set.
   Fix: replace the three cards with one real product view, e.g. the Plan overview (vendor tracker plus timeline) as an app screenshot with a two-line caption.
4. **R-06 tracked uppercase in the invitation preview** ("TOGETHER WITH THEIR FAMILIES", letter-spacing 2). Kept on purpose: printed Indian wedding invitations set this line in spaced capitals. No code change.
   Fix: record the reason in `DESIGN.md` (R-31).

## LOW (Quality Locks and copy)

5. **R-15 CTA starting with "Explore"**: "Explore wedding vendors" on the login screen and the website.
   Fix: "Browse vendors without signing in" (login) and "Browse wedding vendors" (website).
6. **Copy vocabulary "unlock"**: chat block message "Book in-app to unlock Booking Protection."
   Fix: "Book in-app to get Booking Protection."
7. **R-21 / R-31 light-only theme without a written reason, and today's design decisions are not recorded.**
   Fix: add to `DESIGN.md`: light only because the identity is wedding stationery on ivory paper; Fraunces for display only; burgundy plus gold as the two core colours, green and red only for status; the dials above; today's changes (roadmap, calendar, compact vendor cards, solid-mandap icon).
8. **antislop-code decorative separator comments**: six `// ---- auth …`, `// ---- vendor profile`, etc. in `server.ts`.
   Fix: plain sentence-case comments (comments only, no code changes).

## Checked and passing

- R-01, R-07, R-10, R-13 no gradients, grid backgrounds, glass or glow (the burgundy cards are flat fills).
- R-03 / R-25 tap targets 44 pt or larger (smaller pills carry hitSlop); text contrast at least 4.5:1 after today's colour fix.
- R-04 custom wedding-specific SVG icons; the star is used only for ratings.
- R-16 no buzzwords in UI copy.
- R-17 no invented statistics; counts come from the API.
- R-19 one 300 ms entrance, press feedback, reduced motion respected.
- R-27 lists have empty, loading and error states.
- R-29 two core colours plus status colours.
- R-39 roles and labels on controls; decorative icons hidden from screen readers.
- Design canvas mockups use `href="#"` on some tabs. These are static mockups, verified by inspection, so they do not ship.
